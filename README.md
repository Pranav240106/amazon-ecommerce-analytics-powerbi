# Amazon E-Commerce Analytics | Power BI

An end-to-end **E-Commerce Business Intelligence and Analytics solution** developed using **Power BI, DAX, Power Query, and Python** to analyze business performance, customer behavior, pricing, demand patterns, revenue concentration, and future sales outlook.

The project transforms transactional e-commerce data into an interactive, decision-oriented dashboard designed to provide a structured view of business performance and customer intelligence.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

E-commerce businesses generate large volumes of transactional data across customers, products, orders, pricing, discounts, payments, and geographic markets.

The objective of this project was to transform this data into a **centralized analytical solution** that helps understand:

- Overall business performance
- Customer purchasing behavior
- Customer value and segmentation
- Product and category performance
- Pricing and discount behavior
- Demand and order patterns
- Revenue concentration
- Payment method contribution
- Future sales outlook

Rather than treating each analysis as a standalone report, the dashboard is structured as a progressive analytical journey:

**Business Performance → Customer & Market Intelligence → Revenue, Pricing & Demand Analytics**

---

# 📊 Dashboard

The Power BI solution consists of three analytical pages.

## 1. Executive Business Overview

The first page provides a consolidated view of the overall business.

### Key areas analyzed

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Customers
- Average Order Value
- Profit Margin
- Category-wise sales performance
- Country-wise order distribution
- Top-performing products
- Monthly sales and profit trends
- Geographic sales distribution

### Objective

To provide an executive-level understanding of the current business performance and identify major sales, profitability, product, and geographic patterns.

---

## 2. Customer & Market Analysis

The second page focuses on customer behavior and market dynamics.

### Key areas analyzed

- Customer purchasing frequency
- Repeat vs. new customers
- Customer segmentation
- Customer revenue contribution
- Segment-level performance
- Geographic customer patterns
- Payment method preferences

### Objective

To understand customer purchasing behavior, identify differences between customer segments, and analyze how customers contribute to overall revenue.

---

## 3. Revenue, Pricing & Demand Analytics

The third page focuses on commercial performance and demand-related analysis.

### Key areas analyzed

- Monthly demand seasonality
- Discount sensitivity
- Profit margin behavior
- Order/basket size distribution
- Product revenue concentration
- Revenue contribution by payment method
- Top customer revenue concentration
- Future sales forecast
- Loss-making SKU identification

### Objective

To examine the relationship between pricing, demand, profitability, and revenue concentration while providing a forward-looking view of sales.
